Extract audio from video files:

- In the Audio tab, enable Extract audio from video to add MP4, MOV, and M4V files.
- Original audio (no re-encoding) is selected by default. The first audio track is saved in a matching format, such as M4A for AAC or ALAC, preserving its encoded audio data.
- Turn off Original audio to convert to MP3, Ogg, Opus, FLAC, or WavPack.
- Extract files in batches with input filters and your chosen output destination. Source videos are preserved.
- Videos without audio report an individual failure while other files in the batch continue.
